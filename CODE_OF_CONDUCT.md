# Code of conduct

AutoMode follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is not a replacement for it. If the two differ, the Covenant applies.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or role. We pledge to keep this a place that is open, friendly and free of harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Say thanks when someone reviews your change.
- Help new contributors. A first pull request is a big step.
- Keep discussions on topic, and keep private information private.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Trolling, or posting someone's private information.
- Anything that a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a professional space.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. They will explain a decision when they can.

To report a problem, open a private advisory under the **Security** tab of this repository and say it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers see it. We will treat the report as private, and we will not take action against anyone for reporting in good faith.
